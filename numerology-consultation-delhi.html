What's The Real Deal About Numerology?

Numerology is based on two things really: your birthday and your name. Your birthday gives you your life path number (that's set in stone), which shows your natural strengths and the patterns you'll fall back on. And your name? Well, that gives you your destiny number and psychic number - and that's the vibration that your name is really putting out there, which is pretty different from your birthday date. The spelling of your name can actually be adjusted, which is why this is the most common request people have.

The reason people come looking for name correction in particular is because your birth number is fixed so if it feels like its working against you, adjusting your name's spelling is the one lever that numerology offers.

Chaldean and Pythagorean are two widely followed numerology systems. Chaldean numerology connects letters with specific number values, while Pythagorean numerology assigns numbers according to each letter’s place in the alphabet.

Numerology and Astrology: Key Differences Explained

The thing is, numerology and astrology are often lumped in together but they actually work on entirely different ideas. Astrology tries to make sense of what the planets were up to when you were born. Numerology, on the other hand looks at the numbers in your birthdate and the spelling of your name. Loads of people have been known to use both because they each give you a different kind of information - they're not competing with each other, but rather filling in different gaps in your picture.
