Vastu Consultation in Delhi

Make Your Space More Balanced & Comfortable

A house or office layout can greatly affect how you feel when you're in it. Vastu Shastra is an old system that looks at what direction your rooms are in, where they are in your home, and where you put your furniture - all with the aim of making the space feel more at home and peaceful.

Saroj Jawa can do Vastu for homes, offices, shops and pretty much any kind of business. It's based on your actual floor plan and the positioning of the rooms in relation to the outside. He'll be looking for practical things rather than a complete reworking.
