What Your Kundli Report Shows

A kundli (birth chart) maps the position of the planets at the exact moment you were born. This checker generates that chart and breaks it down into sections you can actually read, not just raw astrological data.

Your kundli report includes:

  • Graha positions – where each planet sat in the sky at your birth
  • Lagna (ascendant) – the rising sign, used as the base point for the entire chart
  • Rashi (moon sign) – your moon sign, often used for daily predictions
  • Nakshatra – your birth star, used in matching and personality reading
  • Dosh identification – checks for manglik dosh, kaal sarp dosh, and other common doshas
  • Kundli milan score – if you're checking two charts for marriage matching

Why Birth Time Matters

The lagna and house positions shift roughly every two hours. If you don't know your exact birth time, the chart can still be generated using your date and place of birth, but the lagna and house-based readings won't be fully accurate. If your birth certificate or hospital record doesn't have the time, birth time rectification is a separate, more involved process — this tool gives you a good starting chart either way, but flags where precision depends on time.

Kundli Checker vs Manual Pandit Reading

An online kundli checker and a manual reading from a pandit serve different purposes — one isn't a replacement for the other.

  • Online kundli checker: generates the chart, positions, and dosh status instantly, based on standard calculation methods. Useful for a first look, for comparing two charts before matchmaking, or for understanding your own chart before a consultation.
  • Manual pandit reading: adds interpretation — what a specific dosh means for your situation, remedies, and context the calculation alone doesn't give.

If you want a quick, no-cost check before deciding whether to book a detailed consultation, the online checker is the right starting point. If you need interpretation or remedies, that's a separate, more personal step.
