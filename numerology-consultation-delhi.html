What Numerology Actually Looks At

Numerology works from two things: your date of birth and your name. Your date of birth gives your life path number — a fixed number that doesn't change and points to your natural strengths and the kind of challenges you tend to repeat. Your name gives your destiny number and psychic number, which numerologists read as the "vibration" your name carries — and which can be adjusted, unlike your birth date.

This is why name correction is the most common request: your birth number is fixed, so if it's working against you, spelling adjustments to your name are the lever numerology offers.

Two systems are commonly used — Chaldean numerology, which assigns number values based on the sound of letters, and Pythagorean numerology, based on their position in the alphabet.

Numerology vs Astrology

Numerology and astrology are often confused because both are used to explain personality and timing, but they work from different inputs. Astrology reads planetary positions at the time of your birth. Numerology reads the numbers in your birth date and name. Many consultants — including numerology-focused practitioners — use both together rather than treating them as competing systems, since they answer slightly different questions.
