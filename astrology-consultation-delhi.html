How it All Works

It all starts with your kundli (birth chart) which is constructed from your date, time and place of birth. The chart shows where all the planets were in the sky at the exact moment you were born, and a good astrologer in Delhi will then go on to interpret the relationships between the planets to give you a much greater insight into the patterns in your career, marriage and finances.

Saroj Jawa draws on two rich traditions to inform her practice:

  • Vedic astrology — This is the traditional Indian system, based on the sidereal zodiac which takes into account the slow wobble of the Earth - so if you get a Vedic reading, it might differ from a Western sun-sign reading for the same birth date.
  • Lal Kitab — This is a separate system that's all about providing simple, easy-to-implement remedies that you can incorporate into your daily life rather than elaborate rituals to counterbalance planetary issues.

Vedic Astrology vs Western Astrology

The two systems differ in more ways than just style: they're based on different zodiacs. Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac (based on the seasons). Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac (which is adjusted to where the stars actually are these days), and that's been shifted since the tropical zodiac was originally set up. That is why your Vedic sun sign might differ from your Western one, and why Saroj Jawa, as a Vedic astrologer, might interpret the same birth details differently from a Western astrologer.
