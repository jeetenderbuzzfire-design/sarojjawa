About Saroj Jawa

15+ Years of Experience in Numerology, Astrology, Vastu Shastra & Spiritual Guidance

A Journey of Knowledge, Experience & Meaningful Guidance

For over 15 years, Saroj Jawa has been dedicated to helping individuals gain deeper understanding, clarity, and direction through the wisdom of Numerology, Astrology, Vastu Shastra, Spiritual Healing, and Life Guidance.

Throughout her journey, she has worked with individuals seeking answers during different stages of life—from making important career and business decisions to understanding relationships, planning significant milestones, creating more balanced living spaces, and navigating periods of personal change.

Her approach begins with a simple understanding:

Every Person Has a Unique Journey.

No two individuals have the same experiences, circumstances, or questions. This is why Saroj Jawa believes meaningful guidance should never follow a one-size-fits-all approach.

By combining years of experience with personalized attention, she aims to offer insights that are clear, relevant, practical, and meaningful to each person's journey.

Experience Built Through Years of Dedicated Practice

A meaningful practice is built not only through knowledge, but also through years of learning, observation, experience, and understanding people.

Her work includes helping individuals explore questions related to:

Career & Professional Growth

Understanding strengths, opportunities, important transitions, and professional decisions.

Business & Important Decisions

Guidance related to business names, new ventures, timing, and important professional choices.

Marriage & Relationships

Exploring compatibility, relationships, marriage-related questions, and important personal decisions.

Home & Workplace

Applying Vastu principles to homes, offices, business spaces, new properties, and renovations.

Our Philosophy

Guidance Is Not About Telling You What to Do. It Is About Helping You See More Clearly.

Saroj Jawa believes that meaningful guidance should empower you with greater understanding rather than create fear or dependency.

The purpose of a consultation is not simply to focus on predictions. It is about helping you understand your patterns, possibilities, circumstances, and choices from a deeper perspective.
