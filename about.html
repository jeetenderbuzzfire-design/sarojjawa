About Saroj Jawa

Over 15 Years of Experience in Numerology, Astrology, Vastu & Spiritual Guidance in Delhi

Guidance Shaped by Experience, Understanding & Trust

For more than 15 years now, Saroj Jawa has been working as a numerologist and astrologer based in Delhi, helping people make sense of their lives with Numerology, Astrology, Vastu Shastra, Spiritual Healing and Life direction. She has spent all this time sitting down with folks at all sorts of points in life - choosing a career, making a big business call, trying to get a handle on a relationship, wedding planning, moving into a new place or office, or just trying to navigate through a tough time. Each one had a different problem, and she tackled each as a one-off challenge.

Her approach starts from a pretty simple place:

Every person's life journey is one of a kind.

You can't find two people who are an exact match - same name, same birth details, same life story, same worries. So Saroj Jawa doesn't do cookie-cutter answers; every reading starts with you and your own story.

By combining extensive experience with a person-centered approach, she explains each insight in a way real people can understand- no jargon, no mumbo-jumbo, so you can think about what it all means and take the next step with real confidence.

Experience Built Through Years of Devoted Practice

After years of honing your skills, you start to notice the tiny details most people miss the first time around. That's basically the essence of Saroj Jawa's work as a numerologist and astrologer in Delhi. She's learned to tune in, listen, and get to the heart of what someone truly wants.

The kinds of questions people come to Saroj for help with include:

Career & Professional Growth

Figuring out where your strengths lie, when it's the right time to leap, and what to do when it comes to big career decisions.

Business & Important Decisions

She can help with business name ideas, timing for new ventures, and making tough calls on major business decisions.

Marriage & Relationships

She can help with compatibility, the ins and outs of kundli matching, and other marriage-related questions.

Home & Workplace

She can show you how to use Vastu principles to make the most of homes, offices, shops, and renovations.

Our Philosophy

Guidance Is Not About Telling You What to Do. It Is About Helping You See More Clearly.

Saroj Jawa believes that meaningful guidance should empower you with greater understanding rather than create fear or dependency.

The purpose of a consultation is not simply to focus on predictions. It is about helping you understand your patterns, possibilities, circumstances, and choices from a deeper perspective.
