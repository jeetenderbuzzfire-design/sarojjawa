What You Can Expect from Your Kundli Report

A kundli (birth chart) is like a blueprint of the planets at the exact moment you were born. We use this free kundli checker to create that chart and break it down into bits you can actually understand – not just a load of complicated astrological numbers.

Your kundli report includes:

  • Each planet’s placement in the sky when you were born.
  • Your lagna (ascendant), used as a starting point for the whole chart
  • Your rashi (moon sign), the same one you see in daily horoscopes
  • Your birth star (nakshatra), used to match personalities and more
  • Dosh identification – whether you need to watch out for stuff like manglik dosh or kaal sarp dosh
  • A kundli milan score, if you're looking to see how well two charts match up for marriage

Why Getting the Right Birth Time is So Important

The lagna and house positions shift roughly every couple of hours so if you don't have your exact birth time then the chart will still be generated from your DOB and place, just the more specific lagna and house predictions won't be 100% accurate. If you're missing your birth certificate (or it doesn't have the time on it) then you'll have a bit of a problem in terms of getting an accurate reading and in this case you'd need to go through a process known as birth time rectification. This free kundli checker gives you a good starting point, though, and shows where the time difference really matters.

Kundli Checker vs a Real Pandit Reading

A free kundli checker and a reading done by a professional pandit have two very different roles - and they can't really replace each other.

  • Free kundli checker: spits out the chart, planetary positions and dosh status in the blink of an eye, using tried-and-tested calculation methods. It's useful for getting a first look, comparing two charts if you're thinking about getting married, or just understanding your own chart before you start looking for advice.
  • A real pandit reading brings in the interpretation - and that's where the magic happens. They can explain what a specific dosh means for your situation, which remedies are worth considering, and all the context that the chart itself just doesn't have.

If you want a no-cost check to see if a more in-depth consultation is for you, the free kundli checker is a good place to start. But if you need someone to help interpret the results and give you some concrete advice, that's a whole different story.
