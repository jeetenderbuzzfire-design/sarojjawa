What Your Name Numerology Report Shows

Numerology assigns a number to every letter in your name. Add them up using a set method, and you get your name number — a value believed to carry its own energy and influence.

Your report includes:

  • Name number – the total value of your current name, calculated letter by letter
  • Destiny number – derived from your full birth name, said to reflect long-term life direction
  • Lucky alphabet – letters considered favorable additions if you're correcting or adjusting a name
  • Name vibration – the overall energy reading tied to your name's number combination
  • Name-birth date compatibility – how your name number lines up with your date of birth, if you enter both

Chaldean vs Pythagorean Numerology

There are two common systems for calculating name numbers, and they don't always agree. Chaldean numerology assigns number values based on sound and vibration and is the older of the two systems; Pythagorean numerology assigns values in a fixed alphabetical sequence and is more common in Western numerology. This checker uses the Chaldean method. If you've had a name number calculated elsewhere using a different system, don't be surprised if the number doesn't match — that's expected, not an error.
